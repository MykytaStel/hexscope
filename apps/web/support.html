Support hexscope

hexscope is free and open source, with no ads, no accounts and no tracking, and it will stay that way. It is made by one person. If it kept something private that you were about to send, here is how to help it.

1 Thank the developer

Any amount, once or every month. It pays for the domain and, more than that, for the time to keep hexscope fast, careful and free.

    2 Help without money

    • Tell someone who sends photos or documents: a colleague, a parent, a friend selling something online.
    • Write to the developer about a file it read wrong, or one it should read: , no account needed.
    • Star it on GitHub, which helps others find it: github.com/MykytaStel/hexscope.
    • Translate it into your language, or improve a translation, on GitHub.

    3 What it will not do

    Support changes nothing in how hexscope treats you: everyone gets every feature, your files stay in your browser, and nothing about you is kept or sold. There are no investors to answer to — only the people who use it.
